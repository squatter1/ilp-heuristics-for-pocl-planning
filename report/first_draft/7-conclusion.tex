\chapter{Conclusion}\label{chap:conclusion}

We introduced new heuristics for POCL planning which account for all information within the partial plan they are calculated on. These are the first POCL delete-relaxation heuristics to not ignore information that has already been found by the search process. Our empirical evaluation showed that they have the potential to significantly reduce the size of the search space, at the cost of high runtimes. We then described how to compute these heuristics by using ILP models. While our heuristics weren't competitive with the state of the art in any domains, they were more informed than alternative heuristics for all domains tested. As we created the first prototype of a new class of POCL heuristics which don't relax their search node information, there is a large array of potential future work which can take this theoretical groundwork and build more efficient practical implementations of it. Our report discusses some of these promising lines of future research and how they could improve the effectiveness of ILP-based delete relaxation heuristics for POCL planning, as well as discussing the possible application of our techniques to optimal planning.
