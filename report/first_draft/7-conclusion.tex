\chapter{Conclusion}\label{chap:conclusion}

We introduced new heuristics for POCL planning which account for all information within the partial plan they are calculated on. While these heuristics are NP-complete and slow to compute, they have the potential to significantly reduce the size of the search space. These are the first POCL delete-relaxation heuristics to not ignore information that has already been found by the search process. We then described how to compute these heuristics by using ILP models. While our heuristics weren't competitive with the state of the art on any domains, they were more informed than alternative heuristics on all domains tested. Our report also discusses promising lines of future research which could improve the effectiveness of ILP-based delete relaxation heuristics for POCL planning, and adaptations which could allow our techniques to be adapted to optimal planning.
